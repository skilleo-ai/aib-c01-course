# AIB-C01 course blueprint

Editorial draft, 14 November 2026. Independent exam preparation; no AWS endorsement claimed.

## Depth and learning design

39 domain lessons across 13 modules, targeting about 7.8 hours of video, plus a 10-minute orientation and a 30-minute capstone. Allow 4–6 additional hours for exercises and revision. These are planning estimates; clarity determines final duration. Domain teaching time is 108/144/108/108 minutes. Exam weighting guides scope, not a rigid allocation of minutes.

Every lesson must include a measurable decision outcome, prerequisites, a worked original business example, a failure or counterexample, a learner decision, explained feedback and sources. Explain tradeoffs and evidence quality; avoid service-name memorization. Revisit three cases: customer support, document processing and workforce transformation. Introduce distinct industries to test transfer.

Orientation covers the audience, how to use worksheets and the current official exam guide. Keep changeable exam logistics in a separately maintained resource. The capstone requires a strategy memorandum, economics worksheet, risk register and phased adoption plan for a fictional organization.

## Assessment plan

At least 117 lesson questions (3 per lesson), 4 domain reviews of 12 questions, and 2 independent 50-question original practice forms: 265 items planned in total. Practice forms allocate 12/14/12/12 items to domains 1–4. This is an instructional design choice, not a claim about the number of questions on the real exam. A separate item-review pass checks ambiguity, distractor plausibility, scenario sufficiency, skill mapping and answer leakage. Current pilot questions are drafts, not psychometrically validated items.

A mapped skill is planned coverage, not proof of instruction. A skill is complete only when instruction, example, assessment and expert review are complete. All 39 lessons need expert review; only the pilot currently has a narration draft.
