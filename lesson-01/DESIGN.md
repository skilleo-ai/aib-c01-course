# Lesson visual identity

## Style Prompt
A warm, conversational business classroom on a deep navy canvas. Clear hierarchy, generous space, readable diagrams and restrained reveals help a broad audience follow the reasoning. Teal marks learned patterns and amber marks generated content or decision points. Use warm white for primary text. Keep technical precision without a futuristic or promotional tone. User direction: warm and conversational, dark mode, broadly accessible theme colors.

## Colors
- #101D2C — canvas
- #1C3044 — raised panels
- #F5F0E8 — main text
- #7BE0CC — teal emphasis
- #FFD08A — amber emphasis
Secondary text uses #CBD6DF. Accent fills use the dark canvas for readable text.

## Typography
Inter for headings and body; locally bundled with its OFL license. Headlines 68–84 px, body 32–40 px, diagram labels at least 28 px on a 1920×1080 canvas.

## Motion
Brief 0.5–0.7 second entrances, predictable reading order, gentle 0.35-second crossfades between scenes. Diagram steps follow the explanation. No background loops. Narration measured before final scene timing. Captions stay within a dedicated lower safe area.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon glow, stock robots or sci-fi decorations.
- No dense paragraphs on screen.
- No color-only distinctions.
- No tiny screenshot text or decorative background motion.
- No claims of AWS endorsement or official practice questions.
