## Source nuances to preserve

- Beta duration, question count and pricing come from the live beta listing. The general guide lists a different duration; preserve that distinction.
- Early Adopter eligibility is based on earning the certification by 15 February 2027, not merely registering. Recheck before recording or publication.
- The marketing page emphasizes portable business judgment. The exam guide nevertheless recommends strategic awareness of AWS tools; do not turn that into either an implementation lab or a claim that AWS context never appears.
- The 2026 McKinsey figures use different measures: personal productivity and reported enterprise EBIT impact. Do not present them as a causal comparison or a single success-rate funnel.
- The certification map is not a mandatory progression. Technical credentials require different practical preparation.
