### Slide: Why learn AI business strategy?
<!-- id: m0-aib-purpose-value -->
<!-- type: content-image -->
<!-- visual: image -->

Welcome. This course develops business judgment for AI adoption. AWS emphasizes taking AI from adoption to scale. The focus is evaluating AI investments, building business cases, designing governance, and scaling adoption. The goal is to connect AI decisions to measurable outcomes, across organizations and industries. Why learn it? These skills help you decide where AI fits, justify investment, manage risk, and expand what works. We will practice that reasoning, rather than promise a particular career outcome.

- Build business judgment
- Evaluate value, risk and adoption
- Apply skills across organizations

### Slide: Who is this certification for?
<!-- id: m0-aib-audience-landscape -->
<!-- type: content-image -->
<!-- visual: image -->

This is for people who lead or influence AI outcomes: product and program managers, business leaders, analysts, consultants, and marketing or sales professionals. You do not have to build the models yourself. AWS recommends basic AI familiarity and six months alongside AI initiatives. Coding, implementation experience, and another certification are not required. AI Practitioner validates foundational AI concepts and AWS AI service knowledge. Business Strategist focuses on choosing investments, building business cases, managing risk, and driving adoption. Earning both is optional.

- Business and cross-functional roles
- AI familiarity; no coding prerequisite
- Different purpose from AI Practitioner
