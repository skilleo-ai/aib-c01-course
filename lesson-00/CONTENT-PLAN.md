# Lesson 0 — two-slide recording-first review

## Slide 1: Why learn AI business strategy?

Objective: explain AWS's positioning and the practical reason to learn the subject.

Primary page: https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-business-strategist/

Review and record the certification heading and positioning, the introductory business-decision summary, then open the career-value FAQ. Emphasize evaluating investments, business cases, governance and scaling useful adoption. Do not promise promotions, salary gains or guaranteed exam success. Avoid beta logistics in narration because these can change.

## Slide 2: Who is it for, and where does it fit?

Objective: let a learner judge audience fit and distinguish this credential from AI Practitioner.

On the same AWS page, open “Who should take this exam?” and record role examples plus recommended experience and prerequisites. Then open “How is this different from AWS Certified AI Practitioner?” and record AWS's own comparison. Do not invent a required certification ladder. The comparison is between strategic business judgment and foundational AI/ML/GenAI plus AWS AI service knowledge. AWS states that earning both is optional.
